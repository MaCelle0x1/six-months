# Six Months

A grounded, command-line survival RPG about relationships, choices, and the collapse of society during a fictional pandemic.

## Concept

A fictional neurological disease begins spreading across the American Midwest. The player starts as an ordinary person, alone, shortly before society begins to collapse.

Over approximately six months, the player's decisions shape who they become, who survives, which relationships endure, and what kind of communities emerge around them.

## Design Pillars

- **People over numbers:** relationships are multidimensional and expressed through behavior rather than visible friendship meters.
- **Grounded survival:** combat, injuries, weapons, resources, and the outbreak remain grounded in reality.
- **The world moves without you:** time, infrastructure, communities, quests, and characters change independently of the player.
- **Emergent identity:** there are no fixed classes; the player's role develops through background, skills, abilities, and choices.
- **Consequences without constant punishment:** important actions are remembered and can matter much later.

## Current Status

Pre-alpha / active development.

The current repository contains the project's design documentation. The implementation will be developed incrementally, beginning with the core game state and command-line loop.
